<h1 align="center">Obsidian Legion</h1>
<p align="center"><strong>Your ideas. Your systems. Your rules.</strong></p>
<p align="center">Independent minds. Shared ambition.</p>

We are building a meeting ground for programmers, systems engineers, and system architects who want to create on their own terms.

Bring your curiosity, your unfinished idea, or a problem worth solving. Build tools you understand, infrastructure you can operate, and systems you can evolve. Share what you learn so the next builder can go further.

## Start here

- [Visit the website](https://wolfbreed1829.github.io/obsidian-legion/).
- [Introduce yourself and meet other builders](https://github.com/obsidian-legion/community/discussions).
- [Propose a project](https://github.com/obsidian-legion/community/issues/new?template=build-idea.yml).
- [Read the community guide](https://github.com/obsidian-legion/community#readme).

## Find your discipline

| Discipline | What we build |
| --- | --- |
| Programmers | Useful software, developer tools, and local-first applications |
| Systems engineers | Home labs, automation, observability, and resilient infrastructure |
| System architects | Clear boundaries, documented tradeoffs, and systems designed to evolve |

## What we value

**Sovereignty.** Understand your tools. Own your decisions. Keep the freedom to change both.

**Craft.** Care about the details, from the first function to the last failure mode.

**Shared knowledge.** Document the process. Share the lessons. Give the next builder a head start.

Public discussions are open to GitHub users. Organization and team membership are managed by the owner; participation does not require repository write access.

**Build for yourself. Not by yourself.**
